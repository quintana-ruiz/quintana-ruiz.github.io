Welcome

Osvaldo Dario Quintana Ruiz

Doctor of Structural Engineering

I am an Associate Researcher at the Computational Mechanics Laboratory and currently a member of the Structural Engineering Research Group (GIIE), Faculty of Engineering, National University of Asunción.

About

I am from Paraguay, residing in Asunción. I hold a bachelor's degree in architecture (2005) and a master's degree in civil engineering (2013) from the National University of Asunción. I earned my Ph.D. in structural engineering from the University of São Paulo, Brazil (2021) under the supervision of Prof. Eduardo M. B. Campello.

My primary research focuses on simulating and applying machine learning algorithms to optimize 3D-printed systems. I developed my own artificial neural network (ANN) program fully implemented in a low-level language, and other programs for multiphysical analysis of particle systems.

In 2014, I received the TOYP Prize (Ten Outstanding Young People in the World) in the category of academic achievements.

Topics of interest granular materials · additive manufacturing · computational solid mechanics · discrete element method · machine learning · genetic algorithms.

Research

I have experience in the following topics:

  • Computational simulation of 3D concrete printing
  • Computational simulation of selective laser sintering (SLS) processes
  • Machine learning and genetic algorithms
  • Hydration heat and thermal stresses in concrete structures
  • Heat transfer in particle systems
